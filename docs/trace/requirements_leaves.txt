requirements_leaves.txt

1.1.1.1 Internal source interfaces shall expose values as generalized PQ streams.
1.1.2.1 Internal transform kernels shall consume and produce exact state over generalized PQ semantics.
1.1.3.1 Internal composition shall preserve generalized-stream semantics until final client emission.

1.2.1.1 Any RCF-compatible source shall be representable as a PQ stream with q=1.
1.2.2.1 RCF adapters shall not introduce alternate arithmetic semantics distinct from PQ semantics.

1.3.1.1 Internal components shall not expose speculative client-visible RCF digits directly.
1.3.2.1 Any internal RCF-like prefixes used for feedback or adaptation shall remain non-client-facing until certified.

1.4.1.1 Built-in named sources shall enter the system through generalized-stream-compatible adapters.
1.4.2.1 Unary, binary, and diagonal kernels shall interoperate through exact stream/state contracts.

2.1.1.1 Each emitted client term shall be an exact integer RCF term.
2.1.2.1 Client-visible emission shall occur only at the certified-output boundary.

2.2.1.1 A term shall not be exposed to the client merely because it appears likely.
2.2.2.1 Internal narrowing without proof shall not justify client-visible output.

2.3.1.1 Once emitted, a client-visible term shall remain valid under all later refinement.
2.3.2.1 Later source ingestion shall not invalidate earlier client-visible terms.

2.4.1.1 Certification shall consider the full current admissible image, not a sample point.
2.4.2.1 Equality of floor across the admissible image shall be sufficient for emission.
2.4.3.1 Failure to prove floor stability shall prevent emission.

3.1.1.1 BLFT coefficients shall be maintained using exact integer arithmetic only.
3.1.2.1 BLFT state transitions shall preserve exactness under ingest and emission steps.

3.2.1.1 Binary operators shall be representable through BLFT state over two inputs.
3.2.2.1 Unary operators that are linear-fractional shall be representable as BLFT specializations.

3.3.1.1 DLFT coefficients shall be maintained using exact integer arithmetic only.
3.3.2.1 DLFT state transitions shall preserve exactness under Y-term ingestion and coefficient update.

3.4.1.1 The Newton refinement map for sqrt shall be encoded as a DLFT.
3.4.2.1 The square-related unary path may use DLFT-compatible machinery where required.

3.5.1.1 The rectifier shall observe the current admissible image before emission.
3.5.2.1 The rectifier shall block output when certification conditions are not met.

3.6.1.1 Internal progress may continue without client-visible emission.
3.6.2.1 Rectification shall preserve exactness and non-retractability guarantees.

3.7.1.1 Normalization shall not change the mathematical transform represented by the kernel.
3.7.2.1 Normalization shall not introduce division by non-common factors.

4.1.1.1 No source shall be preread solely to simplify implementation.
4.1.2.1 Source consumption shall be justified by a current certification or refinement need.

4.2.1.1 A certified term shall not be withheld once the next-output condition is met.
4.2.2.1 Emission readiness shall be reevaluated after each state-changing step.

4.3.1.1 Constructing an operator pipeline shall not require exhausting its inputs.
4.3.2.1 Lazy behavior shall hold for finite, rational, and streaming irrational inputs.

4.4.1.1 When multiple ingests are possible, the chosen ingest shall be justified by exact range information.
4.4.2.1 Any equivalent ingest policy shall preserve correctness and certified-output semantics.

4.5.1.1 Repeated runs from the same state shall make the same ingest choice.
4.5.2.1 Determinism shall hold absent external randomness or non-deterministic resource failure.

5.1.1.1 Public observation of current range shall not require future source consumption.
5.1.2.1 Reported current range shall reflect the exact current admissible set.

5.2.1.1 Endpoint values shall be represented without floating-point approximation.
5.2.2.1 Endpoint comparison shall use exact rational ordering.

5.3.1.1 Each endpoint shall carry open/closed status explicitly.
5.3.2.1 Openness shall participate in normalization and equality rules.

5.4.1.1 Outside ranges shall be first-class values, not approximated as large finite intervals.
5.4.2.1 Projective-line semantics shall remain consistent through transform propagation.

5.5.1.1 Corner images shall be computed exactly from admissible endpoint data.
5.5.2.1 When the image is disjoint, exact union semantics shall be preserved.

5.6.1.1 Denominator zeros within the admissible domain shall be detected exactly.
5.6.2.1 Crossing-induced outside-range results shall be represented explicitly.

5.7.1.1 Normalization shall not collapse outside-ranges into lossy finite surrogates.
5.7.2.1 Certification logic shall account for outside-range structure when computing floors.

6.1.1.1 EOF collapse shall use the appropriate projective tail interpretation for the exhausted side.
6.1.2.1 EOF collapse shall preserve exactness of the remaining live transform.

6.2.1.1 Unary/binary collapse shall remain expressed through the existing kernel state when feasible.
6.2.2.1 Exhaustion handling shall not introduce ad hoc non-kernel semantics.

6.3.1.1 When the admissible set is a single exact rational, rational-collapse entry conditions shall hold.
6.3.2.1 Rational collapse shall not begin before exact singleton isolation is proven.

6.4.1.1 The emitted finite sequence shall equal the exact RCF of the isolated rational.
6.4.2.1 After the final term, the stream shall terminate cleanly.

6.5.1.1 Lack of immediate progress alone shall not justify silent looping.
6.5.2.1 Resource-limit failure shall be explicit and observable to the caller.

7.1.1.1 Pi generation shall use an exact continued-fraction-compatible construction.
7.1.2.1 E generation shall use an exact continued-fraction-compatible construction.

7.2.1.1 PQStreamFromRCF shall convert an exact RCF prefix or stream into PQ terms.
7.2.2.1 Exact rational values shall be injectable through dedicated rational-source adapters.

7.3.1.1 Reciprocal shall preserve exactness and certified emission semantics.
7.3.2.1 Square shall preserve exactness and integrate with interval-based certification.

7.4.1.1 Sine-in-radians support shall remain exact and lazily composable.
7.4.2.1 Tanh support shall remain exact and lazily composable.

7.5.1.1 Core shall not encode source-specific hacks for named constants.
7.5.2.1 Core shall not encode trig-specific heuristics or identities.

7.6.1.1 Degree inputs shall be converted exactly to radians before sine evaluation.
7.6.2.1 The degrees wrapper shall not alter the underlying certified-output contract.

7.7.1.1 Shortcut paths shall not violate laziness where laziness is required by contract.
7.7.2.1 Shortcut paths shall not bypass certification guarantees.

8.1.1.1 The procedure shall operate using exact arithmetic only.
8.1.2.1 The procedure shall return a rational witness admissible under the exact interval contract.

8.2.1.1 Open and closed endpoints shall be respected exactly.
8.2.2.1 Outside-range or projective cases shall be handled when valid under the interval model.

8.3.1.1 Rational-collapse logic may use the 101C utility to identify canonical witnesses.
8.3.2.1 Use of the 101C utility shall preserve exact termination behavior.

8.4.1.1 White-box and black-box tests may use 101C witnesses for exact expectations.
8.4.2.1 Diagnostic output may report 101C-selected witnesses when helpful.

9.1.1.1 The caller shall be able to bound evaluation effort.
9.1.2.1 Guard configuration shall not weaken correctness guarantees.

9.2.1.1 Kernel or state growth may be bounded explicitly by configuration.
9.2.2.1 Growth-limit handling shall fail explicitly rather than silently truncating exact state.

9.3.1.1 Term-count limits, if provided, shall be enforced without violating emitted-term correctness.
9.3.2.1 Term-count exhaustion shall surface as an explicit stop or error condition.

9.4.1.1 Exhaustion errors shall be distinguishable from ordinary EOF termination.
9.4.2.1 Exhaustion errors shall not expose uncertified partial output as certified.

9.5.1.1 Failure shall not retroactively invalidate previously emitted certified terms.
9.5.2.1 Failure handling shall not substitute approximate results for exact ones.

12.2.1.1 Deferred decimal support shall not distort the exact-RCF-first architecture.
12.2.2.1 Decimal-oriented work may be postponed without weakening current exactness requirements.

12.3.1.1 Future Hurwitz-related work shall remain subordinate to current correctness requirements.
12.3.2.1 Future Hurwitz-related work shall not require architectural reversal of the current exact-kernel design.

12.4.1.1 Diagnostics should help explain current kernel coefficients and admissible ranges.
12.4.2.1 Diagnostics should help explain why an ingest or emission decision was taken.

12.5.1.1 Future sharing or memoization shall not require weakening the no-duplicate-consumption invariant.
12.5.2.1 Future sharing or memoization shall remain compatible with exact lazy evaluation.

12.6.1.1 Demonstration of HAKMEM 101A-style best-approximation properties shall not require speculative client-visible output.
12.6.2.1 Documentation of HAKMEM 101A-style best-approximation properties shall remain consistent with certified-emission semantics.

12.7.1.1 The target expression shall be supported through the exact lazy operator stack rather than by a one-off shortcut.
12.7.2.1 Success on the target expression shall remain downstream of the foundational signoff requirements above.
