sqrt_requirements_leaves.txt

10.1.1.1 Sqrt shall use exact arithmetic only and shall not use floating-point seeding or refinement.
10.1.2.1 Sqrt shall consume radicand terms on demand rather than prereading the input.
10.1.3.1 Client-visible sqrt output shall consist only of certified RCF terms.
10.1.4.1 The inner feedback loop shall be named ourorobos and shall be white-box visible.

10.2.1.1 The emerging output prefix shall serve as the live approximation stream fed back into refinement.
10.2.2.1 The sqrt controller shall not duplicate-consume a live stream to simulate BLFT composition.

10.3.1.1 The Y approximation stream shall be ingested into the DLFT as the live variable stream.
10.3.2.1 The radicand X shall contribute through coefficient selection or update rather than as a second live DLFT stream.

10.4.1.1 The controller shall implement Y_{n+1} = (Y_n + X/Y_n) / 2.
10.4.2.1 For rational X = n/d, the controller shall encode refinement as the DLFT (dY^2 + n) / (2dY).
10.4.3.1 The DLFT coefficients for rational X shall be initialized as (A=d, B=0, C=n, D=0, E=2d, F=0).

10.5.1.1 BLFT-only composition shall not be accepted as an equivalent implementation of the Newton kernel.
10.5.2.1 The 1/2 factor of Newton refinement shall be represented in DLFT state rather than by a separate required half stream.

10.6.1.1 Sqrt shall not determine finiteness by exhausting X up front.
10.6.2.1 EOF, if reached, shall be handled through ordinary core collapse semantics.

10.7.1.1 Sqrt shall not special-case named sources as a substitute for the general feedback design.
10.7.2.1 Any exact rational shortcut retained shall remain explicit and bounded by documented requirements.

10.8.1.1 If X exhausts before sqrt resolution completes, standard core tail-to-infinity collapse shall apply.
10.8.2.1 EOF handling shall not discard accrued sqrt-controller progress.

11.1.1.1 A nil input stream shall produce an error.
11.1.2.1 A current range that proves X < 0 shall produce an error.
11.1.3.1 A current range that allows non-negative values shall permit evaluation to proceed.
11.1.4.1 An exact X = 0 input shall emit 0 and terminate.
11.1.5.1 Any PQ term with P < 0 or Q < 0 shall produce an error.
11.1.6.1 The exact error surface shall be consistent with the rest of the core API.

11.2.1.1 The initial seed shall be a strictly positive exact rational.
11.2.2.1 Seed selection shall not require lookahead beyond currently available X information.
11.2.3.1 Allowed seed strategies include a value derived from the current X interval or the exact rational value 1.
11.2.4.1 The selected seed shall be compatible with Newton convergence.

11.3.1.1 Each emitted term shall be fed back immediately after certification.
11.3.2.1 The ourorobos shall accumulate the certified term and the range certified at that point.
11.3.3.1 No uncertified term shall be appended to the ourorobos.

11.4.1.1 Before the first emitted term, the active Y approximation shall be the seed.
11.4.2.1 After feedback begins, Y terms shall come from PQStreamFromRCF(ourorobos).

11.5.1.1 The implementation shall maintain a split representation or exact equivalent separating n,d dependence from Y-derived factors.
11.5.2.1 When X is refined from n/d to n'/d', the controller shall update the X-dependent part of DLFT state without reinitializing the full DLFT.
11.5.3.1 Refining X shall not discard already ingested Y-term progress.

11.6.1.1 A term shall be emitted only when the rectifier proves a single integer floor over the full admissible image.
11.6.2.1 Certification attempts shall be observable in white-box tests.

11.7.1.1 The controller shall first attempt certification from the current DLFT-plus-rectifier state.
11.7.2.1 If certification fails, the controller shall ingest the next Y term from the ourorobos or seed path.
11.7.3.1 If Y is exhausted and certification still fails, the controller shall ingest one more X term, refine the X estimate, and update DLFT state.
11.7.4.1 If the ingest budget is exceeded before certifying the next term, the controller shall return an explicit error.

11.8.1.1 SmallestRationalInInterval shall be used for nontrivial intervals.
11.8.2.1 Any selected n/d used for sqrt coefficient state shall satisfy n/d > 0.

11.9.1.1 sqrtController.ourorobos shall be directly observable in white-box tests.
11.9.2.1 sqrtController.hasOurorobosFeedback shall be directly observable in white-box tests.
11.9.3.1 The DLFT state or coefficients after each Newton step shall be directly observable in white-box tests.
11.9.4.1 The seed approximation before feedback begins shall be directly observable in white-box tests.
11.9.5.1 Whether certification was attempted with the current state shall be directly observable in white-box tests.

11.10.1.1 sqrt(0) shall produce exact 0.
11.10.2.1 sqrt(1) shall produce exact 1.
11.10.3.1 sqrt(4) shall produce exact 2.
11.10.4.1 sqrt(1/4) shall produce exact 1/2.
11.10.5.1 Negative exact input shall produce an error.
11.10.6.1 sqrt(2) for 50 terms shall match OEIS A040000, beginning [1, 2, 2, 2, 2, ...].
11.10.7.1 The live-feedback path for sqrt(2) shall be confirmed by white-box tests rather than by shortcut behavior.

12.1.1.1 Where sqrt-specific docs conflict with older project notes, the Gosper-centered governing specs shall win.
12.1.2.1 Superseded design notes shall not override current approved requirements.
